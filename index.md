    <h1>Qi Yin, Ph.D.</h1>
    <p><strong>Research Associate</strong><br>
    Carnegie Institution for Science</p>

    <p>
    I am a developmental biologist investigating the cellular and molecular mechanisms 
    governing oocyte development and follicle dynamics, and how these processes influence female fertility.
    </p>

    <p>
    In Dr. Allan Spradling’s laboratory, I identified distinct functional waves of ovarian follicles 
    and demonstrated that wave 1 follicles primarily promote theca cell formation and androgen production 
    rather than directly supporting fertility.
    </p>

    <p>
    My current research focuses on nuclear positioning and acentriolar microtubule-organizing center 
    (acMTOC) dynamics during oocyte growth and meiotic preparation.
    </p>

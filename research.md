# Research

## Follicle Wave Dynamics and Ovarian Physiology

I identified distinct waves of ovarian follicles in mice and demonstrated that early wave 1 follicles remodel to promote theca/interstitial cell expansion and steroidogenic activity.

## Nuclear Positioning and Cytoskeletal Organization

My work investigates nuclear centralization, cytoplasmic reorganization, and acentriolar microtubule-organizing center (acMTOC) dynamics during follicle growth and meiotic preparation.

## Long-Term Research Vision

My long-term goal is to define the regulatory mechanisms governing oocyte maturation from the primordial follicle stage through full growth and meiotic maturation across species.
